About me

Curious about data, focused on insight

I'm a data analyst who enjoys the whole journey from a messy spreadsheet to a dashboard someone can act on. I write SQL to query and shape data, use Python (pandas, NumPy and Matplotlib) for cleaning and exploratory analysis, and build Power BI reports with DAX and Power Query to bring the findings to life.

My recent work covers hotel booking performance, the UK Index of Multiple Deprivation 2025, and audience ratings on Rotten Tomatoes. Each project starts with a clear question and ends with a clear answer.
